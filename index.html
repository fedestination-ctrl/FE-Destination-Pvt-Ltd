FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Studying & Working Abroad

Common questions students and professionals ask before starting their international journey.

How do I start my study abroad journey?

Book a free consultation with FE Destination in Gulberg III, Lahore. We evaluate your profile, discuss your goals, and create a step-by-step roadmap.

Can I study abroad without IELTS?

Yes. Many universities in Europe, Malaysia, and the UAE accept Medium of Instruction (MOI) letters or offer English proficiency waivers depending on your previous education.

Do you help with student visa applications?

Yes. We provide complete student visa filing support, document review, financial documentation, and mock interview preparation for UK, Europe, Australia, and other destinations.

How much does it cost to study abroad from Pakistan?

Costs vary by country. Europe offers low-cost public universities, while UK and Australia have higher tuition but strong post-study work opportunities. Book a consultation for a personalized budget estimate.
