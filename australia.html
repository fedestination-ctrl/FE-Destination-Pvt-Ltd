Study in Australia 🇦🇺

🎓 Entry Requirements

  • Academic transcripts matching university criteria.
  • IELTS (Overall 6.5 bands) / PTE equivalent accepted.
  • Genuine Temporary Entrant (GTE) or Genuine Student (GS) requirement.
  • Financial capacity proof for tuition and living.

🏙️ Famous Study Hubs

Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, and Perth offer top-ranked global universities, excellent part-time work rights, and strong post-study work visa opportunities.
