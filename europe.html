Study in Europe 🇪🇺

🎓 Entry Requirements

  • High School or Bachelor's Degree depending on the program level.
  • IELTS (6.0+ bands) or alternative English Proficiency Proof / Medium of Instruction (MOI).
  • Blocked Account / Financial Proof as per country regulations.
  • Motivational Letter & Academic CV.

🏙️ Famous Study Hubs

🇪🇺 Schengen Countries (e.g., Germany, France, Italy, Netherlands, Sweden): Offer world-class public universities with low/zero tuition fees. A single Schengen visa allows travel across multiple European countries.

🌍 Non-Schengen Countries (e.g., Ireland): Exceptionally high-standard education systems with great post-study work opportunities and unique immigration frameworks.
