Study in USA 🇺🇸

🎓 Entry Requirements

  • High School Diploma or Bachelor’s Degree depending on the program level.
  • TOEFL, IELTS, or Duolingo English Test (DET) proficiency scores.
  • Standardized Tests (SAT/ACT for undergrad or GRE/GMAT for grad programs, if required).
  • Financial Proof / Bank Statement and Statement of Purpose (SOP).

🏙️ Famous Study Hubs

🇺🇸 Top Study Destinations (e.g., California, New York, Massachusetts, Texas): Home to world-renowned Ivy League and public research universities offering cutting-edge programs, unmatched campus life, and global networking.

💼 Post-Study Opportunities: F-1 visa holders get opportunities for Optional Practical Training (OPT) for up to 1-3 years depending on their STEM degree major.
