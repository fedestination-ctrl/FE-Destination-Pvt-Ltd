Who We Are

Your Trusted Partner for International Education

Foreign Education Destination (FED) is an education consultancy based in Lahore, Pakistan, supporting students who want to pursue higher education internationally.

With more than seven years of international recruitment experience, our focus is on providing practical guidance throughout the study-abroad journey — from choosing the right destination and university to preparing applications and navigating the student visa process.

We work with students to understand their academic background, goals, budget and preferred destination before helping them explore suitable study options.

  • Profile-based guidance
  • University & course selection
  • Application assistance
  • Student visa guidance
  • Scholarship guidance
  • Pre-departure support

Our Direction

What Drives Foreign Education Destination

Our work is centered around helping students understand their international education options and move forward with greater clarity and confidence.

Our Mission

To provide students with clear, personalized and responsible guidance for international education. We aim to simplify the process of exploring universities, preparing applications and understanding student visa requirements.

Our Vision

To become a trusted education consultancy known for professional service, transparent communication and student-focused guidance, connecting ambitious students with opportunities around the world.

Our Values
